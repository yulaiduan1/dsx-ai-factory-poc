The DSX Pillars

An AI Factory rests on three vertically integrated pillars. This walkthrough focuses on the middle pillar — facilities infrastructure.

Pillar 01

DSX Hardware

Racks and Network

Compute (GPU racks — e.g. Vera Rubin), East-West network (Spectrum-X), North-South network, and storage. Cabinet TDP scales from MGX Gen 1.1 (198 kW) through Gen 1.2 Vera Rubin NVL72 (330 kW) up to Gen 1.3 (630 kW single rack, 660 kW with paired Power Rack).

Pillar 02 — In focus

DSX Facilities Infrastructure

Campus, Structures, Power and Cooling

Electrical and cooling — chip-to-grid and chip-to-atmosphere. Building structures, controls, and fiber pathways. Everything you see on the site plan to the left.

Pillar 03

DSX Software

Infrastructure as a Service Layer

Power management (DSX MaxQ & Flex), operations (observability, break-fix), provisioning (Bare Metal Manager), and networking automation. DSX Exchange brokers data between layers.
